NYE Pier Party at Pier One

A heritage wharf right under the Harbour Bridge with a party, two dinner seatings and a lounge, so you can pick the price and pace that suits you.

What's included

  • Four-hour premium beverage package (Pier Party)
  • Six+ food stations
  • Headline DJs
  • Early ($349) and late ($699) dinner seatings at PIER
  • Harbour Bridge Lounge from $499

About NYE Pier Party at Pier One

NYE Pier Party at Pier One is at Pier One Sydney Harbour, 11 Hickson Rd in Walsh Bay (Circular Quay & The Rocks). It's one of the parties, harbourfront, dinners options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: Both 9pm & midnight. Age: 18+.
