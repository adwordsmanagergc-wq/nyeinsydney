About NYE at MCA Rooftop

NYE at MCA Rooftop is at Museum of Contemporary Art, Level 6 Harbourside Room in The Rocks (Circular Quay & The Rocks). It's one of the parties, rooftops options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: Both 9pm & midnight. Age: 18+.
