About Lower Bradfield Park Ticketed Vantage

Lower Bradfield Park Ticketed Vantage is at Lower Bradfield Park in Milsons Point (North Shore). It's one of the ticketed vantage points, family-friendly, under $250 options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: Both 9pm & midnight. Age: All ages.
