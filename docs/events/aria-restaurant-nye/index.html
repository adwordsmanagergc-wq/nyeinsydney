New Year's Eve 2026 · Circular Quay

New Year's Eve at Aria

Fine dining with floor-to-ceiling views of the Opera House and Bridge. The early sitting is one of the best-value harbour dinners in the city.

What's included

  • Early sitting ($320pp)
  • Cocktail party with canapés & beverages ($650pp)
  • Six-course tasting menu with wine pairing ($1,950pp)

About New Year's Eve at Aria

New Year's Eve at Aria is at Aria Restaurant, 1 Macquarie St in Circular Quay (Circular Quay & The Rocks). It's one of the dinners, fine dining options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: Both 9pm & midnight. Age: All ages (sitting dependent).

Planning the rest of your night? Check the fireworks timeline and transport tips or compare vantage points around the harbour.
