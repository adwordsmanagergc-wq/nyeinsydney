About Ring in the New Year at Sydney Tower Eye

Ring in the New Year at Sydney Tower Eye is at Sydney Tower Eye, 100 Market St in Sydney CBD (Sydney CBD). It's one of the ticketed vantage points, family-friendly, under $250 options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: 9pm or midnight session. Age: All ages; SKYWALK 10+.
