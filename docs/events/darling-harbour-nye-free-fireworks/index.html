About New Year's Eve at Darling Harbour (free)

New Year's Eve at Darling Harbour (free) is at Cockle Bay, Darling Harbour in Darling Harbour (Darling Harbour & Barangaroo). It's one of the free, family-friendly, ticketed vantage points options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: Both 9pm & midnight. Age: All ages.

Still deciding? Compare it with full NYE in Sydney directory or browse vantage points around the harbour. For the night itself, check the fireworks timeline and transport tips.
