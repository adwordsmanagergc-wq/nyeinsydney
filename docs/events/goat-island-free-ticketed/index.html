About Goat Island (free ticketed)

Goat Island (free ticketed) is at Goat Island / Me-mel in Sydney Harbour (On the Harbour). It's one of the ticketed vantage points, free options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: Both 9pm & midnight. Age: All ages.
