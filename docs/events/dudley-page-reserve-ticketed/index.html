About Dudley Page Reserve Ticketed Vantage

Dudley Page Reserve Ticketed Vantage is at Dudley Page Reserve in Dover Heights (Eastern Suburbs). It's one of the ticketed vantage points, family-friendly, under $250 options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Sydney. Fireworks: Both 9pm & midnight. Age: All ages (under 18s with a ticketed adult).
