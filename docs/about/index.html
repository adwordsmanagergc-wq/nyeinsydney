What we do

NYE in Sydney brings every way to see in the new year on Sydney Harbour into one place. That means harbourside dinners, fireworks cruises, rooftop parties, family picks and the official vantage points, each with its price, what's included and which fireworks you'll see.

Start with the NYE in Sydney directory, then filter by area, budget or type. Every listing links straight to the venue to book. We don't take commission, so the price you see is the venue's price.

Independent, not official

We're an independent guide. The fireworks and official vantage points are run by the City of Sydney, which publishes the final program in December. We follow its announcements and update our planning guide and vantage points map as details are confirmed.

For venues

If you run a New Year's Eve event in Sydney, you can list it for $599. You get your own event page, a place in the directory and a direct booking link, with no commission.
