Midnight AEDT, Thursday 31 December 2026

Being on the water is the closest you can get to the fireworks barges. NYE cruises normally board in the late afternoon, include dinner and drinks, and stay out for both the 9pm and midnight displays. Boats take up positions in exclusion zones set by NSW Maritime, so the operator's licence and position matter.

Not sold on the water? Browse every NYE in Sydney event, from harbourside dinners to free vantage points.

Booking tips

  • Check where the vessel will sit for the midnight show.
  • Compare boarding wharf and return times, as transport after midnight is busy.
  • Smaller capacity means more deck space at midnight.
  • Most NYE cruises are 18+. Check before booking for families.
