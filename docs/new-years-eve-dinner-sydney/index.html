Midnight AEDT, Thursday 31 December 2026

A harbourside table is the most comfortable way to watch the fireworks: no camping out, no crowds, and a long dinner while you wait for midnight. Early sittings usually include the 9pm show and cost far less, while late sittings take you through to the midnight fireworks.

Want to compare a dinner with a cruise or a party? Our NYE in Sydney guide puts every option side by side.

Booking tips

  • Book early. The best harbourfront tables sell out months ahead.
  • Early sittings are the best-value way to get a harbour view, and many include the 9pm show.
  • Ask whether your table is guaranteed a fireworks view or only the venue is.
  • Allow extra travel time, as road closures start in the afternoon.
