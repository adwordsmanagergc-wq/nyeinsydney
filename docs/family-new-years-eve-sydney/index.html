The 9pm Family Fireworks, ticketed parks with facilities, Taronga Zoo and early dinners.

Midnight AEDT, Thursday 31 December 2026

The 9pm Family Fireworks were designed for kids, so you can see a full harbour show and be home in bed before midnight. Ticketed parks give you guaranteed space, toilets and food, which makes a big difference with little ones.

For grown-up plans after the kids are asleep, see all the NYE in Sydney events in one place.

Booking tips

  • Aim for the 9pm Family Fireworks with younger kids.
  • Ticketed parks guarantee space and facilities, so you don't need to arrive at dawn.
  • Pack hats, sunscreen, water, a picnic rug and ear protection for little ones.
  • Agree on a meeting point in case anyone gets separated.
